# Privacy Policy — Kioskeo

**Last updated:** September 23, 2026

## Introduction

Kioskeo ("the App") is developed by EgeaINC. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

## Your Business Data Stays on Your Device

**EgeaINC does not collect, store, or receive your business data.** Products, sales, clients, cash closings and settings are stored only on your device.

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**:
- **Products:** Product names, barcodes, prices, stock levels, and images (stored in local SQLite database)
- **Sales:** Sale records, payment methods, and amounts (stored in local SQLite database)
- **Clients:** Client names, phone numbers, and account balances for store credit (stored in local SQLite database)
- **Categories:** Product category assignments (stored in local SQLite database)
- **Preferences:** App settings like theme and business name (stored in SharedPreferences)

This data never leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

## Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. Users with Kioskeo PRO do not see ads, and the App does not start the ads service for them.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:
- The device's advertising ID
- Approximate location derived from the IP address
- Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
- Ad interactions (impressions and taps)

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not by EgeaINC. Your business data (products, sales, clients) is never shared with AdMob.

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Your choices:**
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
- Subscribing to Kioskeo PRO removes all ads.

## Camera Usage

The App uses the device camera for barcode scanning to identify products. **Images are processed on-device only using Google ML Kit and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.**

## Internet Usage

The App connects to the internet for:

1. **In-app purchases:** Processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not have access to your payment information. Google's privacy policy applies to these transactions.
2. **Ads (free version only):** Loading ads from Google AdMob, as described above.

## Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:
- **Camera:** Used for scanning product barcodes. Images are processed on-device only and are never recorded or transmitted.
- **Internet:** Required for in-app purchases through Google Play and for loading ads in the free version.
- **Advertising ID:** Used by Google AdMob to show ads in the free version.
- **Storage:** Used for importing product lists (PDF/Excel/CSV) and exporting/importing database backups. Files are processed locally only.

## Backup & Data Export

The App allows you to export your database as a backup file. This file is shared using Android's native share functionality — you choose where to save it (Google Drive, OneDrive, etc.). **EgeaINC does not have access to your backup files.**

## Children's Privacy

The App is a point-of-sale tool for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
